About Sun Handicraft

Where wood becomes heirloom furniture

Sun Handicraft specialises in quality wooden furniture and handcrafted designs — from carved sofas and Maharaja chairs to dining sets, beds and cabinets. Every piece is built by hand in solid wood, combining traditional Indian carving craft with finishes suited to modern homes.

We work directly with skilled artisans, choosing seasoned wood and time-tested joinery so each item is strong enough for everyday life and beautiful enough to be passed down.
